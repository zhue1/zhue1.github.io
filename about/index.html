About

Who I am

I'm a computer science senior at the University of Florida and the founder of Orbi Labs. I build software end to end and ship it to real users: mobile apps, backends and the AI between them. I want to build tools people can rely on to get real results.

Right now I'm the sole engineer on Ora, an AI training coach for iOS, Android and the web, with 700+ merged pull requests and 86 Cloud Functions. To work like a small team, I built EricOS, an agent-orchestration platform where parallel LLM coding agents ship reviewed pull requests. I'm also Chief of Operations for the IEEE Signal Processing Society at UF, a 450+ member club that hosts speakers from NVIDIA and Microsoft.

I grew up in Miami helping at my family's restaurant after school, and taught myself to code at 11. At 15 I built a multiplayer RPG on Roblox by myself: 7,000+ lines and 58 animations. In high school I competed on the robotics and computer science teams.

I'm looking for Summer 2027 software engineering internships in backend, data and full-stack. If your team is building something that matters, email me at imericzhu@gmail.com.
